Recupero — app redesign
The full app in the refined dark system, pushed further. Two directions per screen: A · Refined keeps each screen's structure but cleans hierarchy, spacing and the decay/XP language; B · Reworked pushes the layout — category colour accents, pill filters, card-based timelines. Day·Logs metadata (your pain point) is rebuilt as tidy labelled wells that never truncate.
